AAbout

From auditing financial institutions to working inside one

I've spent three years inside the numbers of South African asset managers and banks. Now I want to work on the other side of the table.

At Deloitte I trained in the Financial Institutions Services audit team. By my third year I was lead senior on audits covering almost a quarter of South Africa's collective investment scheme market, running a team of nine and coordinating with our team in India. I was also part of the audit of a major bank, and worked on the audits of a state-owned small-business funding entity and one of the country's largest retailers.

I took the long way into the profession. I started at SA Home Loans straight after school, handling client queries and loan calculations, then went back to the University of Johannesburg and passed both SAICA board exams on the first attempt. Seeing financial products from the client's side before auditing them still shapes how I work.

I also take a practical interest in how AI is changing finance work, and I test new tools with the same scepticism I'd apply to audit evidence. I write about it in my journal.
